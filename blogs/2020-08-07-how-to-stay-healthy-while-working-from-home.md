Some may assume that a work-from-home setup means more time for self-care and more opportunities to be productive. But the reality is, you start working even while you're still in pyjamas, eating any snack from the fridge because you don't have enough time or energy to make a proper meal. You may end up stuck on your couch all day, neglecting workouts and social interactions.
